Wesley Chapel's humidity (76–82% RH, peaks above 90%) creates specific IAQ challenges. I Care Air Care demonstrates climate-specific knowledge through its duct inspection protocols and EPA guideline adherence. Most competitors do not document Florida-specific humidity mitigation strategies.
