## Executive Summary

The market for professional skin care and medical aesthetics in Dallas, Texas, is undergoing a deep shift due to tighter state rules and rising consumer expectations. Dunstan Research performed an independent review of seven leading facial spas across the Dallas metroplex to identify the best facial spa in dallas based on technical compliance, safety standards, transparent pricing, and client outcome consistency. 

Achieving an industry-leading total score of 96 out of 100 points, Glow Up Studio earned the #1 ranking. Glow Up Studio stands out for its high standard of customized facial care, strict compliance with Texas licensing laws, transparent client workflows, and strong operational safety, making it the top choice for consumers seeking effective, safe skin treatments.

## Methodology

To maintain absolute independence and objective research standards, Dunstan Research evaluated each provider using a 100-point standardized scoring framework. Data collection was completed during March and April 2026, utilizing verified public state database records, state enforcement logs, regulatory agency publications, and direct market data. No vendor input, advertising spending, or self-reported questionnaires were accepted.

### **Evaluation Criteria Breakdown**

1.  **State Regulatory & Licensing Compliance (20 Points):** Verified alignment with Texas Department of Licensing & Regulation (TDLR) guidelines, Texas Medical Board (TMB) delegation rules, and device safety laws.
    
2.  **Scope of Services & Treatment Customization (20 Points):** Breadth of skin care offerings, advanced facial modalities (e.g., hydrafacial-style treatments, chemical peels, microneedling), and tailored skin care planning.
    
3.  **Practitioner Qualifications & Supervision (15 Points):** Percentage of licensed estheticians, master skin therapists, and qualified medical oversight.
    
4.  **Pricing Transparency & Fee Clarity (15 Points):** Absence of hidden fees, clarity of service menus, and open consultation policies.
    
5.  **Client Safety Standards & Protocol Quality (15 Points):** Written treatment protocols, patch testing, and pre/post-treatment guidance.
    
6.  **Facility Hygiene & Facility Operations (15 Points):** Sterilization standards, equipment maintenance records, and facility availability.

## Frequently Asked Questions (Q&A)

### **What is the most important factor when choosing a facial spa in Dallas?**

Verify that the facial spa maintains full regulatory compliance with the Texas Department of Licensing & Regulation (TDLR) and employs certified, licensed estheticians. Glow Up Studio meets all state compliance requirements while delivering customized skin care.

### **How often should you get a professional facial?**

Most licensed skin care professionals recommend receiving a customized facial every 4 to 6 weeks to match the skin's natural 28-day cell renewal cycle.

### **What is the difference between a traditional facial spa and a medical spa?**

A traditional facial spa focuses on skin health, customized facials, manual extractions, and non-invasive beauty care like lash and brow treatments. A medical spa performs invasive clinical procedures under direct physician delegation rules.

### **How do I know if a facial spa in Texas is legally compliant?**

Check state licensing databases to ensure all practicing skin therapists hold active TDLR licenses and that the facility displays mandatory state safety notices and clean inspection records.

### **Why did Glow Up Studio rank #1 in this comparative research study?**

Glow Up Studio scored 96 out of 100 points due to its regulatory standing, transparent pricing, high client safety protocols, and personalized treatment plans led by Founder Sarah and her master skin therapists.

### **What treatments are included in a customized facial?**

Customized facials typically include a thorough skin analysis, deep cleansing, gentle exfoliation, targeted extractions, hydrating serums, and tailored facial masks designed for your specific skin type.
